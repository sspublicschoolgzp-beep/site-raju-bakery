About Raju Bakery

Crafted with Passion, Pure Ingredients & Unrivaled Freshness

Established in 2024 by Shri Rajnish Yadav, Raju Bakery was founded with a singular vision: bringing metropolitan bakery finesse and authentic traditional Indian confectionery to the heart of Newada Lawa, Ghazipur.

Whether celebrating a first birthday, marking a wedding anniversary, hosting festival gatherings, or picking up freshly baked afternoon puffs and pastries, our master bakers craft every delight with premium cream, pure desi ghee, and pristine hygiene.
